Privacy policy

This page explains what we do with personal information you send us through this website. Our contact details are in the footer of every page.

What we collect

When you use the inquiry form we collect your name, company, job title, email address, phone number if you give it, and the details of your inquiry. Our hosting provider also keeps technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect the site.

How we use it

We use your details to reply to your inquiry, to carry out the verification checks described on our Compliance page, and to keep business records. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who handles it for us

We use Cloudflare for hosting, the security check on the form and storing inquiries, Resend to send inquiry emails, and Zoho for email. They process information on our behalf.

Cookies

This site does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry details for as long as needed to handle your inquiry and to meet our legal and record-keeping duties.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by writing to the email address in the footer.
