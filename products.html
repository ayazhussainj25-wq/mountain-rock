Terms

Delivery terms, such as FOB, CFR or CIF, and payment terms are agreed between buyer and seller. Payment is normally secured through their own banks.

Quality

Parties usually appoint an independent inspector to check quantity and quality. We do not inspect or certify product, and we do not list prices or available cargoes.
