About this data

Prices are daily spot prices published by the U.S. Energy Information Administration, in US dollars per barrel. They are published with a delay, and our site refreshes them automatically each weekday.

They are not live quotes, and nobody can trade at them. We do not show Dubai, Oman, Murban or product assessments, because price reporting agencies license those.
