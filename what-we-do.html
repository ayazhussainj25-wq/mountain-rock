Two introductions, done carefully.

We work on two links of the supply chain: producer to refinery, and refinery to oil marketing company. In both, our role ends at the introduction and the support around it.

Producer to refinery

We introduce authorised sellers of crude oil to refineries looking for supply. We confirm that the seller is entitled to sell before any details are shared.

Refinery to oil marketing company

We introduce refineries and suppliers of refined fuels to oil marketing companies and importers who need dependable supply.
