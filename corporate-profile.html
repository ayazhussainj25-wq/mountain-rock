Corporate profile

Who we are

Alpine Energy Trading is a brokerage company based in Pakistan. We introduce buyers and sellers of crude oil and refined fuels across Asia Pacific, the Middle East and West and Central Africa.

What we do

We work on two links of the supply chain: producer to refinery, and refinery to oil marketing company. We introduce verified parties and are paid a commission when a transaction they agree is completed.

We do not buy or sell oil on our own account, hold cargo or either party's funds, publish prices as offers, or charge fees before a deal is agreed.

Products and regions

Crude oil, gasoline, gasoil and diesel, jet fuel and fuel oil. Regions: Middle East, West and Central Africa, and Asia Pacific.

How we work

Inquiry, verification, confidentiality and commission agreements, introduction, direct negotiation between buyer and seller, and completion. Buyer and seller contract directly, and payment passes between their banks.

Compliance commitments

  • Verification of each company, its owners and its authority to trade.
  • Screening against UN, US (OFAC), EU and UK sanctions lists.
  • No introductions involving sanctioned parties or sources.
  • No undisclosed intermediaries, bribery or improper payments.
  • Written agreements and confidentiality for every introduction.

Leadership

Alpine Energy Trading acts as an intermediary only. This document is not an offer to buy or sell any product.
