How we work

A clear, documented process

Every introduction follows the same steps. The contract is always made directly between the buyer and the seller.

Standard procedure

From inquiry to signed contract

Procedures vary by product, origin and payment instrument. We confirm the exact steps for each deal in writing.

  1. Inquiry

    The buyer or seller submits their requirement or offer: product, specification, quantity, delivery terms (FOB / CIF / CFR) and payment instrument.

  2. KYC & capability check

    Company registration, authorised signatory and, where appropriate, proof of funds (buyer) or proof of product and capability (seller).

  3. NCNDA & fee agreement

    The parties sign non-circumvention, non-disclosure and intermediary fee terms before any counterparty details are shared.

  4. Document exchange

    The buyer issues an LOI or ICPO, and the seller responds with an FCO or SCO. Documents are checked for consistency and authenticity.

  5. Direct negotiation & SPA

    Principals negotiate and sign the Sales & Purchase Agreement directly. We coordinate communication and timelines.

  6. Inspection & delivery

    Independent inspection (e.g. SGS) and shipment proceed under the SPA and the agreed payment instrument (DLC, SBLC, MT103/TT).

  7. Completion & commission

    Payment passes directly between the buyer's and seller's banks. Our commission, set in writing before the introduction, is paid only when the deal completes.

What we never do

Red lines that protect you

  • We never charge fees before a deal is agreed, and we never hold either party's funds.
  • We never present ourselves as the owner or seller of a cargo.
  • We never share counterparty details without signed NCNDA.
  • We never work with sanctioned origins, vessels or parties.
