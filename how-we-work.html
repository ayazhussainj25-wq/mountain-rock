How an introduction works.

Every introduction follows the same steps, in the same order, for buyers and sellers alike.

  1. Inquiry

    A company tells us what it wants to buy or sell: product, quantity, region and timing.

  2. Verification

    We check the company's registration, the people who own and control it, and its authority to buy or sell. We screen it against sanctions lists.

  3. Agreements

    Each party signs a confidentiality agreement with us before details are shared. Where we are to be paid by a party, it also signs a commission agreement.

  4. Introduction

    We introduce parties whose needs fit, and share only the information they have agreed to share.

  5. Direct negotiation

    Buyer and seller agree specifications, price, delivery and payment terms between themselves and sign their own contract. Payment passes between their banks, not through us.

  6. Completion

    When the transaction completes as agreed, we are paid the commission set out in our written agreement.
