What we do

We bring two parties of a fuel supply chain together, check who they are, and support them until they sign their own contract. The sale is between the buyer and the seller. We are not a party to it.

What we do not do

  • We do not buy or sell oil on our own account.
  • We do not hold cargo or either party's money.
  • We do not publish prices as offers.
  • We do not charge fees before a deal is agreed.
