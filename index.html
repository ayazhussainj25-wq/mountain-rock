About Alpine

We work on two links of the supply chain: producer to refinery, and refinery to oil marketing company. We bring verified parties together and stay with them until they sign their own contract.

01

Direct connections

We introduce principals to principals. There are no long chains of intermediaries and no unnecessary hand-offs.

02

Verification first

KYC, proof of capability and document checks come before any introduction, which protects both sides.

03

Discretion

Commercial details stay confidential under NCNDA. They are shared only with the counterparties who need them.

How we work

A clear, documented process

Every introduction follows the same steps, so that both sides know what happens next.

See the full procedure →

  1. Understand the requirement

    Product, specification, quantity, delivery terms and payment instrument, all confirmed in writing.

  2. Verify both sides

    KYC and company documents, plus proof of funds or proof of product where appropriate.

  3. Introduce under NCNDA

    Principals are connected directly, under signed non-circumvention and confidentiality terms.

  4. Support to contract

    We coordinate documents, inspection and logistics conversations until the SPA is signed between principals.
