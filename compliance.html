Compliance comes before every introduction.

In energy trade, who you deal with matters as much as what you trade. These are the rules we work by.

Who we deal with

  • We verify each company's legal registration and the people who own and control it.
  • We confirm that the company is authorised to buy or sell the product.
  • We deal only with parties we have identified. We do not work through undisclosed intermediaries.

Sanctions

  • We screen companies and individuals against UN, US (OFAC), EU and UK sanctions lists.
  • We do not introduce sanctioned parties, or oil and fuels from sanctioned sources.
  • We decline or stop an introduction when a sanctions concern cannot be resolved.

Money

  • We do not hold or handle either party's funds.
  • We do not charge fees before a deal is agreed.
  • Our commission is set in a written agreement before any introduction.

Conduct

  • We do not offer, give or accept bribes or improper payments.
  • We keep each party's information confidential under a signed agreement.
  • We keep records of our verification and agreements.
  • We may decline or end any introduction at any time.
